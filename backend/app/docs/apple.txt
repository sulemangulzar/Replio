Apple

Apple designs consumer hardware, software, and services such as the iPhone, Mac, iPad, Apple Watch, iCloud, and the App Store. Its products are known for tight hardware and software integration.

Apple focuses on end-to-end product design, combining industrial design, custom silicon, operating systems, and service experiences into a single ecosystem. This approach helps the company create a consistent user experience across devices.

The Apple ecosystem includes services such as Apple Music, Apple TV+, Apple Arcade, iMessage, FaceTime, and iCloud storage. Apple also develops developer tools and operating systems including iOS, macOS, iPadOS, watchOS, and tvOS.

Recent generations of Apple hardware have emphasized performance efficiency, privacy features, and on-device intelligence. The company also uses its own chips in many devices to improve battery life, speed, and integration.
